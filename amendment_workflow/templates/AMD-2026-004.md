# Amendment Proposal: Intent Identity Disclosure & State Identity

**Amendment ID:** `AMD-2026-004`  
**Date:** 2026-04-02  
**Author:** Gateway Global (based on user feedback)  
**Affected Documents:**  
- Technical Manifesto (AIOS-CORE-001) – new Article X  
- Governance Constitution (AIOS-CORE-002) – Article III & VI updates  
- UI Component Registry Standard (AIOS-SPEC-006) – new required component  
- Ecosystem Roadmap (AIOS-REF-012) – Q3 2026 feature line  

**Rationale:**  
The system is intent‑driven, not agent‑driven. The current “intent identity” must be disclosed whenever intent changes. “Agent identity” is replaced by **state identity** — a governed, ephemeral summary of operational mode, skills, and policy constraints.

---

## Proposed Changes (summary)

### 1. Technical Manifesto – New Article X: Intent Identity Disclosure

Insert after Article IX. Defines **State Identity**, disclosure triggers (session start, IntentKey change, material capability change), `aios-intent-identity-card`, audit logging, session intent history, and reinterpretation of “agent” language as **State Identity** / **Intent Drafting Mandate**.

### 2. Governance Constitution – Articles III & VI

- **Article III:** Judicial transparency — Policy evaluations that change `IntentKey` must trigger Intent Identity Disclosure; failure to disclose is escalatable.  
- **Article VI:** Weekly transparency log includes State Identity summary statistics.

### 3. UI Component Registry Standard – Mandatory `aios-intent-identity-card`

Variants: `default`, `compact`, `history`. System-maintained; vendors cannot suppress; DCP rejects Kits that hide disclosure.

### 4. Ecosystem Roadmap – Feature line

**Intent Identity Disclosure (Q3 2026)** — MINOR addition; no breaking Kit changes required.
