## Scope (what this ratifies)

AMD-2026-008 **ratifies**, as a **single governed package**, the **sovereign runtime stack** for **speech-input**, **interpretation**, and **speech-output** by:

| Area | Summary |
|------|---------|
| **Incorporation by reference** | **§3.1** documents (see table above) are **substantive normative law** as of the **ratification date** revision of each, subject to **§3.3**. |
| **Binding obligations** | **§§4–6** — separate lanes (**§4.1**), **no** silent remote substitution (**§4.2**), readiness / disclosure (**§4.3–4.4**), auditability of fallback (**§4.5**), **MPOA** / data classification cross-ref (**§5**). |
| **Informative (not standalone law)** | **WORKSPACE.md** alignment — **§3.2**; **normative** force remains **AIOS-SPEC-007** / **AIOS-SPEC-009** per that section. |

**Non-goals:** This motion **does not** ratify product code; **§7** / **§8** of the amendment remain **informative** sequencing notes. **§4.4** pre-production disclosure is a **post-ratification implementation** obligation in product surfaces, not a blocker on doctrine ratification.
