## Scope (what this ratifies)

AMD-2026-014 ratifies the narrow doctrine that:

- bounded author-like drafting support may use only declared, governed knowledge inputs
- generated prose remains advisory and non-authoritative
- run-record traceability is mandatory for the governed author-like surface
- support standards and implementation artifacts remain subordinate to the amendment's authority boundaries
