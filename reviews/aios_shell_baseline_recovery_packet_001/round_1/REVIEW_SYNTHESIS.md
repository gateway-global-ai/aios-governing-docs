## Convergent gaps

1. The packet still implies governance-gate or work-blocking effects without naming the binding authority, decision-maker, or governing vehicle that would actually create that effect.
2. The packet needs tighter alignment with `AIOS-REVIEW-PACKET-TAXONOMY-001`, especially around formal review/handoff language and packet semantics.
3. The authority trail for the 14 required baseline capabilities must be explicit inside the packet materials, not only assumed from the request context.
4. Repo-relative evidence, freeze metadata, and clearer review mechanics should be tightened so the packet is more portable and more repeatable.
5. The review panel sees the packet as strong evidence scaffolding, but not yet as a fully gate-ready governed packet.
6. The manual Deep Research review adds a second convergent concern: several core baseline flows still remain absent in implementation, especially identity/profile/document flows, so the shell can be extracted as a thin slice but not yet accepted as the full baseline foundation.
7. The additional Gemini manual review strengthens the need to treat taxonomy and authority as first-class remediation targets in the next amendment-plan update, while still not justifying an immediate jump to amendment before packet revision.

## Final adjudication

The seven-review advisory panel converges on three final conclusions:

1. **Problem identification is now correct.** The shell-baseline recovery effort is aimed at the right failure: missing foundation artifacts, weak knowledge-base anchoring, and unstable governance semantics.
2. **The shell itself is real but incomplete.** The current implementation is sufficient to extract and review as a bounded thin slice, but not sufficient to approve as the complete foundation baseline.
3. **The next move remains packet revision, not immediate amendment.** Valid concerns from the manual reviews should be incorporated into amendment planning, but only after the supporting packet is tightened and rerun through review.

The final adopted concern set for the next revision/amendment-planning pass is:

- taxonomy alignment and governed packet semantics
- explicit authority for any gate, freeze, or approval effect
- explicit authority trail for the 14-capability baseline
- repo-freeze and evidence portability discipline
- identity/profile/document foundation gaps
- dual-lane voice and governed agent-control gaps
