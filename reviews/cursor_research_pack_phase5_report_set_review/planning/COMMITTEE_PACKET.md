## 3. Committee findings

### Report-family analysis

The five reports do not do the same job.

- `01_architecture_cartography.md` maps the runtime spine and control-plane candidates.
- `02_implicit_capability_extractor.md` inventories implicit views, actions, routes, and skills.
- `03_governance_breach_audit.md` classifies bypasses and governance failures.
- `04_registry_migration_planner.md` plans what should become registries from the first three passes.
- `05_control_plane_coverage_audit.md` measures how much governance/capability machinery already exists around rules, skills, MCP, and commands.

This is a coherent separated-power research stack, not five redundant notes.

### Current classification problem

The repo's current classification creates one umbrella label for two different realities:

- the five-pass method as an active research pattern
- the specific report bodies as snapshot-bound outputs from a specific tree

Those are not the same thing.

### Committee posture

The strongest committee reading is:

- the methodology should remain active
- the specific report bodies should remain bounded by the tree they observed
- the repo should stop treating the whole family as if active method and historical snapshot are the same thing
