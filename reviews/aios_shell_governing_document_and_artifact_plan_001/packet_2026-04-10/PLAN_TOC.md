## Why this TOC

The order is intentional:

- first freeze source authority
- then define the shell target
- then define artifact classes
- then define the planning chain that is allowed to generate work
- then force review on that chain
- only after that open concrete shell-foundation phases

## Output expectation per section

Each section should produce a bounded phase brief rather than a full artifact burst. The goal is to approve sequence and authority first, not to generate all documents at once.
